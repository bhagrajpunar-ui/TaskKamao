## 1. What TaskKamao is
TaskKamao is a **free rewards (loyalty) program**. You complete simple in-app activities and receive coins. It is
**not** a job, investment, income scheme, bank, wallet, lottery or game of money. **There is no guaranteed or fixed
earning.** Rewards depend on available tasks and partner offers and may change or stop.

## 2. Eligibility
18+ years, resident of India, one account per person, per mobile number and per device. Using emulators, VPNs to
manipulate offers, automation, multiple accounts, or fake referrals is prohibited.

## 3. No deposits
TaskKamao **never accepts money from users**. There is no "Add Money", entry fee or purchase to take part. Nothing in
the app requires payment.

## 4. Coins — fixed ratio
* **100 coins = ₹1.** This ratio is fixed and shown in the app.
* Coins have no cash value until a withdrawal is approved and paid, and cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged

* **Video points** (from watching rewarded video ads) are separate, are **not withdrawable** and are used only for the
  leaderboard.

## 6. Referral program
* Your friend must enter your code when signing up (within 48 hours of signup).
* When your friend has earned **2,000 task coins** (the "eligible activity"), you receive **1,000 coins** and your
  friend receives **500 coins**.
* You also receive a bonus equal to **10% of the task coins** your friend earns from tasks/offers (not from bonuses).
  This is paid by TaskKamao and is **not deducted** from your friend.
* Self-referrals, duplicate devices or fake accounts void referral rewards.
